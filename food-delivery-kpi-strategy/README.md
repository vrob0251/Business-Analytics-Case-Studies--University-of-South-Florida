# Food Delivery KPI Strategy

## Business Problem

A food delivery business needed to improve profitability and increase its contribution to overall company revenue.

## Objective

Define business objectives and KPIs that could help measure progress toward profitability and growth.

## Business Objectives

- Increase revenue
- Improve profitability
- Grow customer retention
- Improve customer satisfaction
- Increase operational efficiency
- Improve driver performance

## Key KPIs

- Revenue growth rate
- Customer satisfaction score
- Customer retention rate
- Average delivery time
- Order accuracy rate
- Completed orders
- New customers
- Profit margin
- Food delivery contribution to total revenue
- Food delivery costs
- Marketing costs
- Driver retention rate
- Driver acceptance rate

## Business Recommendation

Use a balanced set of financial, customer, operational, and driver KPIs to measure whether the business is moving toward profitability and sustainable growth.

KPI Development, Business Analysis, Strategic Thinking, Performance Measurement, Critical Thinking, Business Recommendations
