# Airport Marketing Performance Analysis

## Business Problem

A marketing team needed to determine where to focus a limited advertising budget for the strongest potential return.

The analysis compared airport-level performance using shared-ride passenger volume, marketing spend, and customer ratings.

## Objective

Use Tableau and data storytelling to identify which airport showed the strongest opportunity for additional marketing investment.

## Analysis Approach

- Compared shared-ride passenger volume by airport
- Compared marketing spend by airport
- Reviewed customer ratings
- Evaluated the relationship between marketing spend and passenger volume
- Used Tableau to improve the visual presentation of the data
- Developed a recommendation for the marketing team

## Visualization Improvement

The original pie chart showed the percentage of riders at each airport but did not clearly explain the relationship between marketing spending and passenger volume.

I recommended using a scatter plot because it allows marketing spend and shared-ride passengers to be compared directly. I also added airport labels, filters, and customer-rating color indicators to improve readability and exploration. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

## Business Recommendation

Focus more of the limited marketing budget on JFK.

JFK already showed the strongest passenger activity, and the analysis suggested that additional marketing investment there could help increase monthly users while maintaining a strong customer experience. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}
