# Business Analytics Case Studies

## Overview

This repository contains a collection of business analytics case studies completed as part of my coursework and professional development.

Each case study focuses on a different business problem and demonstrates how analytical tools and business reasoning can be used to support decision-making.

The projects cover areas such as:

- Business problem definition
- Data analysis
- SQL
- Tableau
- KPI development
- A/B testing
- Descriptive statistics
- Data visualization
- Business recommendations

## Case Studies

### 1. Uber Market Expansion Analytics Plan

Developed a business analytics plan to help identify potential markets for Uber expansion.

Focus areas include:

- Market selection
- KPI development
- External data sources
- ETL planning
- Dashboard requirements
- Stakeholder collaboration
- Strategic recommendations

### 2. Compensation Equity Analysis

Analyzed salary differences between two departments using descriptive statistics.

Focus areas include:

- Mean and median
- Department comparison
- Data limitations
- Additional data requirements
- Business recommendations

### 3. Uber Eats A/B Testing Analysis

Evaluated an experiment comparing two versions of an Uber Eats order page.

Focus areas include:

- Click-through rate
- Hypothesis testing
- P-values
- Guardrail metrics
- Experiment interpretation
- Decision-making

### 4. Airport Marketing Performance Analysis

Analyzed airport-level passenger activity, marketing spend, and customer performance to support marketing decisions.

Focus areas include:

- Tableau
- Data visualization
- Marketing analysis
- Data storytelling
- Strategic recommendations

## Tools & Skills

Microsoft Excel, SQL, Tableau, Data Visualization, KPI Analysis, A/B Testing, Descriptive Statistics, Critical Thinking, Business Analysis, Data Storytelling, Strategic Recommendations

## Purpose

The purpose of this repository is to demonstrate how I approach different business problems from an analytical perspective.

Rather than focusing only on technical tools, each case study highlights the full analytical process:

Business Problem  
→ Data / Information Review  
→ Analysis  
→ Findings  
→ Recommendation
