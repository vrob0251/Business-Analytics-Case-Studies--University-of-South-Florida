# Uber Market Expansion Analytics Plan

## Business Problem

Uber is looking to expand into new markets and needs a data-driven approach to identify which cities offer the strongest opportunities for customer and driver growth.

## Objective

Develop a business analytics plan for an executive dashboard that helps prioritize potential expansion markets.

## Proposed Data Sources

- Ticketmaster
- Bureau of Labor Statistics
- National Weather Service
- INRIX
- Uber Investor Reports
- Internal Uber data, when available

## Key Metrics

- Customer acquisition
- Driver sign-ups
- Customer retention
- Customer acquisition cost
- Tourism and event activity
- Traffic and parking conditions
- Market growth potential

## Analytical Approach

1. Define business and stakeholder requirements
2. Identify and collect relevant data sources
3. Extract, clean, and prepare the data
4. Organize KPIs and market indicators
5. Compare cities and states
6. Build an executive dashboard
7. Review findings with stakeholders
8. Validate the dashboard before launch

## Business Recommendation

Use market-level KPIs to identify cities with the strongest adoption potential and prioritize expansion based on customer demand, driver availability, tourism, events, traffic conditions, and other market factors.

The dashboard would help leadership compare potential markets and make more informed expansion decisions.
