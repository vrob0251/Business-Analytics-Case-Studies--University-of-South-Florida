# Compensation Equity Analysis

## Business Problem

A salary discrepancy was reported between two departments performing similar work. The goal was to determine whether the available salary data supported the concern.

## Objective

Use descriptive statistics to compare compensation between departments and determine whether further investigation was needed.

## Analysis Approach

- Compared mean salary by department
- Compared median salary by department
- Reviewed differences in compensation levels
- Considered how outliers could affect the results
- Identified additional information needed before reaching a final conclusion

## Key Finding

The available data showed a difference in average and median salary between the two departments.

However, the salary data alone was not enough to determine whether the difference was unfair.

## Additional Data Needed

- Job titles
- Years of experience
- Tenure with the company
- Hours worked
- Job responsibilities
- Industry salary benchmarks

## Business Recommendation

Investigate the compensation difference further before determining whether a true pay-equity issue exists.

Salary differences should be evaluated alongside employee-level factors such as experience, responsibilities, hours worked, and job title.
